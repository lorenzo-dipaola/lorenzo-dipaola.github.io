## Le dinamiche digitali della rifunzionalizzazione  
**Università di Messina (2021–2023)**

This completed research project explored how cultural and media objects are reshaped, adapted, and reactivated within contemporary digital environments.

The work focused on:

- the migration of content from traditional media into digital ecosystems;
- the reframing of cultural forms through new platforms, formats, and user practices;
- the role of digital technologies in transforming production, circulation, and reception.
